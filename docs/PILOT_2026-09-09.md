# FastCPI source pilot — 2026-09-09

The initial pilot ran three B2B query shapes across DE, DK, EE, FI, FR, LT, LV, NL, PL and SE:

- plain language: A4 80 gsm office copy paper;
- exact identifier: HP W2210A toner SKU;
- procurement service: CPV 72611000 hourly technical computer support.

Exa returned 150 candidates from 134 unique domains. Each category returned 50 candidates;
unique-domain counts were 47 for paper, 43 for toner and 49 for IT support.

The 12 most recurrent domains were fetched through the public-page policy. The baseline generic
JSON-LD/OpenGraph extractor produced four attributable observations. Adding microdata extraction
and the first evidence-driven service adapter increased this to seven. HTTP errors, timeouts,
robots exclusions and pages without public prices remained discovery-only.

Initial recurring domains included HP, Kontorikaubad, OFFICE Partner, Charlot, Orient Office,
Proficient, Kontorsgruppen, Dateks, Staver and Ludwig Office. This is a discovery-frequency pilot,
not a claim that those suppliers are the lowest-priced or representative of the entire market.

The raw dated artifact is stored under ignored `output/pilot/` so volatile page URLs and excerpts
are not treated as a maintained fixture. Parser behavior is covered by deterministic HTML tests.

## Pilot controls and delivery

- Extracted offers retain the source URL, capture time, extraction method, evidence excerpt and
  content hash. A country-code domain that conflicts with the requested market is not ranked as
  an in-market offer; generic domains remain visible with an unverified-domain status.
- Daily watchlist scans persist the observations and threshold events, advance the next daily run,
  and send a source-linked Postmark alert when the owner has enabled email notifications.
- External clients can use separately revocable `prices:read` and `prices:observe` API-key scopes;
  full keys are returned only once and only their SHA-256 digest is stored.
- The live agent contract suite covers plain-language goods, an exact industrial SKU, a broad CPV
  division, hourly services, French-language input and a daily watch request. Answers must retain
  provenance and observed-coverage wording, including when only discovery candidates are found.
